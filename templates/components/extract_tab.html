Steganographic Integrity Limitations

Spatial LSB steganography relies on exact pixel bit preservation. Lossy operations (JPEG recompression, Gaussian blur filtering, spatial scaling/resizing, or cropping) destroy or shift fragile LSB bitplanes. StegoShield reliably detects payload destruction via AES-256-GCM MAC tags, but original secret payloads cannot be mathematically recovered after lossy image alterations.
